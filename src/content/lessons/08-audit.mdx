## Read the opinion precisely

An unmodified opinion is not a guarantee that a business is healthy or fraud-free. It is an opinion on whether the financial statements are presented fairly, in all material respects, under the relevant framework.

Pay attention to the opinion type, going-concern disclosures, and key audit matters. Then find the associated notes and form your own questions.
