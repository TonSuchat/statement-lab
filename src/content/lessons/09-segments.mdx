## A consolidated total can hide the story

Segment data can reveal which businesses drive revenue, profit, assets, and capital spending. Geographic sales can show concentration, currency exposure, or different growth profiles.

Compare segment margins and growth over time. Be careful: companies may change segment definitions, and management’s measure of segment profit may not match consolidated operating profit.
