## A narrative is evidence to test, not a conclusion to adopt

Management discussion can explain drivers, liquidity, known trends, and risks. It is valuable context, but compare every important claim with the financial statements and notes.

Highlight claims about demand, pricing, margins, cash needs, capital spending, and non-GAAP measures. Ask what would falsify each claim.
