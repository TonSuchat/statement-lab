## The one equation worth memorising

**Assets = liabilities + equity.** Every transaction preserves this relationship. Assets are resources controlled by the company; liabilities are obligations; equity is the residual claim of owners.

Use this first: before trusting a figure, identify the reporting entity, reporting period, currency, unit (for example, millions), and reporting framework.

> Teaching heuristic (not a rule): if a number looks surprising, first check the unit, period, and whether it is a stock (point in time) or a flow (over a period).

### Try it

A founder invests $100 cash. Assets increase by $100 and equity increases by $100. No revenue has been earned.
