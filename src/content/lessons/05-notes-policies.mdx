## The statements are the index; notes are the evidence

Notes explain accounting policies, revenue recognition, debt, leases, share-based pay, contingencies, related parties, segment results, and estimates. Read the policy before comparing a metric across companies.

Start with the largest balance-sheet lines and the biggest year-over-year changes. Then locate their note references.
