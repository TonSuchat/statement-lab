## What happened during the period?

The income statement explains revenue earned and expenses incurred during a period. Net income is not the same as cash received: accrual accounting records performance when earned or incurred.

Read vertically for margins and horizontally for multi-year trends. Ask: Did revenue grow? Did gross margin change? Which operating costs grew faster than sales? Are unusual gains or losses included?

### A useful distinction

Depreciation lowers profit now but usually does not use cash in the same period. The cash-flow statement reconciles this difference later.
