## A snapshot, not a movie

The balance sheet is measured at a particular date. Compare several year-ends and connect changes to the cash-flow statement and notes.

Focus on working capital, debt maturity, lease liabilities, goodwill, inventory, receivables, and equity. Liquidity is about the timing of cash obligations—not merely whether total assets exceed liabilities.

### Questions to ask

- Did receivables or inventory grow faster than revenue?
- When does debt mature and at what interest terms?
- Which assets rely on management estimates or impairment tests?
