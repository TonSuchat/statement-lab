## Build a chain of evidence

Compare at least three periods. Link revenue, margins, working capital, operating cash flow, capital expenditure, debt, dilution, and note disclosures. A ratio is a prompt for a question—not a verdict.

### Capstone workflow

1. Establish the reporting basis, period, currency, and business model.
2. Trace the main changes through all three statements.
3. Read the supporting notes, audit report, and management narrative.
4. Record evidence, uncertainty, risks, and questions in the Company Notebook.
