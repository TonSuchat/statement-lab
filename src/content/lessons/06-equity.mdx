## Who owns the change?

Equity changes through profit or loss, dividends, share issuance, repurchases, share-based compensation, and items reported in other comprehensive income. A profitable company can still reduce per-share value if dilution is large.

Compare share count, treasury shares, dividends, and repurchases with cash flow. The statement of changes in equity makes these links visible.
