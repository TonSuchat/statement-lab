## Where did cash actually go?

Operating cash flow starts from business operations. Investing cash flow covers long-lived investments and acquisitions. Financing cash flow covers lenders and owners.

Free cash flow is a useful *analytical measure*, not a universally defined accounting line item. Always write the formula you use and inspect capital-expenditure assumptions.

### Reconciliation habit

When net income and operating cash flow diverge, inspect non-cash charges and working-capital movements. A one-year gap may be normal; a persistent unexplained gap deserves investigation.
